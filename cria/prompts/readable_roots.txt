The harness declares these additional writable roots outside the project: {{ROOTS}}{{BUILD}}. Its active permissions remain authoritative; this note adds no access to other directories. For dependency inspection, use read-only commands there (ls, find, cat, grep, and an archive/binary inspector such as `unzip -l`, `jar tf` or `javap`). To check what an installed library really offers before you code against it, list the archive's contents and print the signatures of the class you will call; `read_file` cannot open an archive.
