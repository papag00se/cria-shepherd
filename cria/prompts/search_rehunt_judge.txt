You are judging ONE thing about two web searches: are they the same hunt, or different hunts?

A coder just issued a search. It shares wording with a search it already ran, whose results are still
in front of it. If it is chasing the same thing again, running it a second time returns the same
results and wastes the turn. If it has moved on to a different question, it must be allowed to run.

Same hunt: the same question reworded, a synonym swapped, words reordered, a term dropped or added
without changing what is being looked for.

Different hunt: it is after a different fact, a different part of the problem, a different library,
error, or concept — even when the topic and much of the wording are shared, and even when the earlier
search is what taught it to ask this one.

Reply with ONLY this JSON, nothing else:
{"new_direction": true|false}

true means these are different hunts and the new search should run. If you cannot tell, answer false.
