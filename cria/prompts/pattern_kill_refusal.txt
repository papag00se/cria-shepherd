A kill that selects by name, pattern or port matches every process on this machine, not only the ones this session started — that has stopped unrelated services here before. Find the specific process id first and kill that: `ss -ltnp | grep :<port>` prints the pid (use `ss` or `lsof -Pi`, not bare `lsof -i`, which prints a service alias instead of the port number), or keep the pid your own start command returned.
