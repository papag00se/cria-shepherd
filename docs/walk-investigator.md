## Original-guidance continuity

These requirements carry forward `docs/walk-prompt.md` at `69c3e9de` (context clues and A → B → C), `ab14571a` (earlier intent and shared logic), `e5ccbcb8` (verify subagent claims), and `381c3b04` (adversarial reasoning and real-call replay); the linked goal at `01dfec38:docs/goals/bonsai-l5-75-loop.md`, §§3–4, makes candidate evidence and harm checks concrete. Those sources establish intent, not an alternate operative process.

- **Context footguns, evidenced in thinking:** follow reasoning to clues about the actual context behind beliefs, decisions, reversals and missed opportunities, not just an application-bug checklist. Look for subtle misleading context as well as obvious defects; cite the recorded thinking that supports the suspected influence, not merely suspicious prompt wording.
- **Recent fixes can help or harm:** check whether recent fixes behave as intended or create further issues in the inspected interactions. Do not assume an existing assist is beneficial. Ask which assist was missing, silent, mistimed or wrong, and why its intervention did or did not land.
- **A → B → C:** if A led to B and C broke, investigate changing A to put B in a better state before guarding B or C. Trace context → recorded thought/action → observed consequence; consider removing a harmful intervention first.
- **Principles are candidate constraints:** apply [design principles](principles.md), including additive/regression-only interventions, ground truth over judgment and task/language/model/harness independence. Check authority, provenance, versions, final-wire delivery and tool facts where relevant. No model blame; an unexplained thought is a question, not a defect verdict.
- **Preserve earlier fixes' intent:** inspect related code and commit history, including old and recent fixes, so a correction does not silently undo a prior protection or reintroduce its footgun. If protections conflict, establish both incidents and resolve their shared cause rather than picking a side.
- **Do not duplicate assist/fix logic:** find similar detection, steering and probe owners; correct, adjust or build on the shared owner instead of adding a competing guard that creates trips and footguns.
- **Adversarial reasoning and real-call validation:** challenge false positives, false negatives and legitimate nearby cases. Validate a proposed correction at the actual intervention boundary using real captured calls and the relevant original/corrected model interaction, with repetitions suited to variance. Tests alone do not establish model-facing effectiveness. If replay requires separately unauthorized inference, leave that validation outstanding rather than claim it happened; report bounded conclusions, not universal benefit.

Subagent suggestions are signals, not established findings: Sol must independently verify claimed chains against original interactions, relevant source/shared owners and executable read-only probes where appropriate. Search is navigation, not proof of absence. Challenge omissions and competing explanations, including prior commitments and stale diagnostics. Distinguish proposed actions from confirmed execution. Do not repeat an entire completed reading to settle a targeted question.

For each legitimate fix candidate state the exact incident evidence and affected calls, upstream owner, falsifiable causal hypothesis, smallest agnostic correction, why existing assists did not prevent it, how the correction can mislead, validation and fails-before/passes-after regression criteria. **No confirmed fix candidate** is a valid result; never invent one.

After a grounded candidate is selected and implementation authorized, Coder owns the cohesive fix and behavioral regression, focused tests and `python -m pytest`; normal review/commit/deployment policy applies. Do not silently promote a walk to a live fix. Keep original reader errors visible in the working evidence; preserve necessary regressions and actual call logs under existing retention policy, without extra approval chains or forensic warehouses.
