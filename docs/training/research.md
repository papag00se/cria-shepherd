# Research basis for bounded decision-model training

Status: research recommendation and offline seed design, 2026-10-24. No training, model inference, GPU work, production changes, or deployment approval occurred in this task. The [archive audit](archive-audit.md) owns authoritative corpus counts, joins, cutoff, and real coverage gaps. The [synthetic-data contract](synthetic-data.md) owns proposed examples and annotation policies. Main owns integration and independent admission review.

## Recommendation, not a universal winner

Compare **Ettin encoder 68M**, **Ettin encoder 150M**, and an **NLI-initialized encoder comparator** on the same independently reviewed, group-split real decisions. Prefer a direct, family-specific classification head as the simplest first experiment for fixed policies; retain an NLI formulation as a comparator, not an assumed winner. Add a SetFit-style comparison only where evidence fits and the sentence-embedding objective is plausible. Evaluate real-only versus real-plus-reviewed-synthetic data separately. Do not infer training sufficiency from this seed or from a benchmark paper.

Keep whole-task completion, independent workspace verification, diagnosis/repair authorship, and memory fidelity requiring broad evidence with the inspecting reasoner. A tiny output vocabulary is not evidence that the underlying judgment is simple. Exact bytes, path identity, provenance, workspace freshness, native context fit, and tool ownership remain deterministic. Do not rebuild deleted outgoing-search relevance. Search-direction examples here concern the distinct overlapping-search/new-hunt question. Planning remains OFF unless explicitly authorized; intake labels do not enable it.

## Primary-source reading and what transfers

Primary paper renderings and the author-maintained cards/repositories identified below were inspected directly, not inferred from search snippets. Paper sections are cited for the claims used; repository/card evidence is distinguished from paper experiments. Browser displays sometimes spill or truncate long renderings; links identify the underlying sources, not a new local archival snapshot. Live model cards/configs were inspected on 2026-10-24; they are recommendations to pin later, not new frozen model contracts. Author claims remain author claims.

### Ettin: controlled architecture comparison, not proof of our task accuracy [ettin2025]

Weller et al., *Seq vs Seq: An Open Suite of Paired Encoders and Decoders*, arXiv:2507.11412v1, especially §§3–4 and Appendices C–E, compares matched encoder/decoder objectives, architecture and data across sizes. Encoders are strong on their evaluated classification/retrieval tasks. Table 6 reports GLUE averages of 87.2 for Ettin-68M and 88.9 for Ettin-150M, versus 88.4 for ModernBERT-base; this is a reason to compare small backbones, not a guarantee that 150M is best for policy interpretation. Their classification results involve task-specific fine-tuning and substantial hyperparameter sweeps. The gain is not a zero-shot score for our labels, and their English evaluations do not establish multilingual/code-switch competence.

The paper's headline “8K” is not an executable window contract: Appendix C and both inspected encoder configs specify **7999** maximum positions. Check the exact checkpoint, tokenizer special tokens, exported graph and runtime together. Neither filling the native window nor increasing it establishes that a classifier uses distant evidence correctly. The model card and paper also differ in some training-phase token descriptions; this recommendation relies on the paper for experimental claims and on the eventual pinned artifacts for runtime properties.

### ModernBERT: code-aware, long-context backbone; hardware and domain qualifications [modernbert2024]

Warner et al., arXiv:2412.13663v1, §§2–4, §6 and appendices, describes masked-language-model pretraining, code-containing data, a modern tokenizer, alternating local/global attention and native 8192 context. Classification and retrieval results come after downstream training, not from an untrained classification head. The paper explicitly limits language claims to English. Long-context single-vector retrieval improves with in-domain long-context tuning; raw context capacity is not sufficient. Efficiency measurements target GPUs, including an RTX 4090, so they do not establish our CPU latency or quantized-export behavior.

Practical consequence: include short and genuinely long **complete** evidence packets in future training/evaluation; test position, attribution and decisive evidence at different locations. Do not copy tutorial truncation defaults or claim that every ModernBERT fine-tune retains 8192. In particular, the previously inspected tasksource NLI artifact has a 2048 contract.

### SetFit: useful few-label comparator, not synthetic-text generation [setfit2022]

Tunstall et al., arXiv:2209.11055, §§3–7 and appendices, fine-tunes a pretrained Sentence Transformer with same-class/different-class sentence pairs, then fits a logistic-regression classifier on the resulting embeddings. Pair construction expands optimization examples; it does **not** create independent observations or independently labeled natural-language documents. The paper evaluates repeated few-shot samples and several multilingual settings with a multilingual backbone. Its main setup uses maximum sequence length 256 and mostly short-text tasks. The paper is not evidence that eight examples per class suffice for long coding histories, asymmetric evidence relationships, or low false-permission rates.

Practical consequence: split original groups **before** creating contrastive pairs, and form pairs only within TRAIN. Do not pair across different policy families just because their numeric labels happen to match. Benchmark fixed-head training against SetFit on the same real group counts, with matched data and multiple seeds; report group counts, not combinatorial pair counts as sample size. Multilingual transfer requires reviewed multilingual evidence, not English-backbone optimism.

### Synthetic classification: generate candidates, then independently verify [zerogen2022; synthetic-modernbert2024]

Ye et al., *ZeroGen: Efficient Zero-shot Learning via Dataset Generation*, arXiv:2202.07922, §§3–4, limitations and appendices, conditions generation on labels and trains a small task model on the generated dataset. Its experiments include classification, NLI and QA; they explicitly expose a diversity-versus-correctness tradeoff and prompt sensitivity, particularly for harder pair tasks. Larger generators and more diverse sampling do not uniformly improve results. Those findings support controlled, reviewed augmentation and real evaluation, not “the requested label must be correct because it conditioned generation.” The implementation's separate correctness evaluator is still a learned proxy, not independent semantic gold.

Berenstein's 2024 author tutorial is a concrete ModernBERT synthetic-domain-classification workflow, not a controlled agent-policy study. It recommends reviewing generated labels, but its random split of one generated dataset and `truncation=True` are **not** our evaluation or evidence contract. Its reported synthetic-domain result does not establish transfer to real coding decisions. Use the workflow as implementation motivation only; do not copy its metrics, training settings, random-row splitting, or truncation as defaults.

The suggested shorthand “GenerateYourLabel” was not resolved to a uniquely verified primary publication during this review. It is not cited as a paper or treated as an established result; the verified ZeroGen paper and concrete synthetic-classification tutorial supply the generation evidence here.

### NLI initialization and label understanding [universal-nli2023; tasksource-card; label-nli-card]

Laurer et al., *Building Efficient Universal Classifiers with Natural Language Inference*, arXiv:2312.17543, §§2–5 and appendices, explains descriptive class hypotheses, mixing NLI and recast classification data, positive/negative pair construction, and held-out-**task** evaluation. Its reported improvement is on its task mix, not a universal margin. It explicitly discusses negative transfer and English-only coverage. Its optional automated cleaning can disproportionately remove minority classes and was not applied indiscriminately to complex NLI/factuality tasks. Its 512-token truncation policy is not acceptable for our complete-evidence decisions.

The tasksource ModernBERT NLI card describes multitask NLI initialization and explicitly distinguishes different task heads. Reported scores across those heads are not all properties of the released NLI head. The zero-shot-label-NLI README describes recasting classification tasks as premise/hypothesis entailment and includes task/license fields and a linked source manifest. This motivates preserving policy identity and license/source identity, not importing that entire mixture blindly. The dataset metadata and live viewer do not suffice to audit every underlying license or duplicate. No external dataset examples were copied into the seed.

Practical consequence: compare raw-backbone task heads against NLI-initialized task heads to isolate initialization from input formulation where possible. A tasksource-versus-Ettin comparison also changes architecture/training mix and must not be called a clean NLI-initialization ablation. If using NLI, feed the complete case and descriptive hypothesis as native text pairs, not chat plus a request to emit a label word. Pin class indices and score rules before evaluation. Neutral is not synonymous with contradictory. Do not manufacture “contradiction” negatives for genuinely ambiguous cases. For fixed policy heads, explicit UNKNOWN and evidence-availability handling may be simpler than multiple hypothesis passes.

### Counterexamples and behavioral variation [hans2019; anli2020; checklist2020]

The HANS authors' complete repository README identifies lexical-overlap, subsequence and constituent heuristics, with per-subcase/per-template evaluation and separate train/evaluation examples. This motivates same-vocabulary, different-intent contrasts; it does not establish that HANS scores measure our policy competence. HANS does not itself justify a claim about negation coverage. Negation, temporal changes, attribution/roles, invariance and directional changes are supported here by CheckList's capabilities and test types.

The ANLI authors' complete repository README describes adversarial collection, independent verifier labels (two verifiers, a third on disagreement), round identities, split discipline, and CC-BY-NC licensing. Its methodology motivates disagreement review and later active-learning collection. Adversarial examples are conditional on the systems that elicited them; they are not population prevalence measurements. No ANLI data was downloaded or added, and adopting an already NLI-trained backbone may carry training-data exposure that cannot be undone by splitting our archive.

Ribeiro et al., *Beyond Accuracy: Behavioral Testing of NLP Models with CheckList*, arXiv:2005.04118, provides minimum-functionality, invariance and directional-expectation tests over a capability matrix. It treats these as complementary to held-out accuracy, not substitutes. Its §5 explicitly says behavioral tests do not settle data versioning, label errors, annotator biases, or worst-case security. Our counterfactual groups adapt that **method**, without copying benchmark sentences: irrelevant formatting/translation should preserve the label when meaning is preserved; changing who is prescribing an action, whether something is a requirement, or whether an exact command is prescribed may change it. Every expected relation still needs semantic review.

### Contamination is a split-design problem, not just exact hashing [dedup2022]

Lee et al., *Deduplicating Training Data Makes Language Models Better*, arXiv:2107.06499, §§4–7, separates exact substring overlap from near-document duplication and evaluates train/validation leakage. It finds that superficial exact deduplication misses templated near copies; its experiments concern generative language modeling, not a numeric prediction of our classifier gains. Its discussion warns that deleting duplicate substrings can delete attribution and that deduplication is not sufficient privacy protection. Adapt the provenance/grouping lesson, **not** a policy of removing repeated passages inside evidence. Here the right unit is retaining an intact document while grouping/quarantining related documents across partitions. Prior knowledge of exposed research documents also remains a disclosure even if no byte-identical input is copied into generation.

### Calibration is not the same as decision accuracy or safe abstention [calibration2017]

Guo et al., *On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks*, arXiv:1706.04599, §§2–5, measures confidence/accuracy mismatch on vision and document classifiers and fits post-hoc calibration on validation data. Temperature scaling is a simple candidate; it preserves the argmax class and therefore cannot repair a semantic misclassification. Its setup assumes training/validation/test from the same distribution, not arbitrary language, harness or future-scenario shifts. We therefore recommend independently checking risk versus coverage, rare consequential errors and subgroup calibration; a low aggregate ECE or a high maximum softmax is not an admission guarantee. Nothing in the paper licenses a particular threshold or a human handback in this system.

## Backbones under a strict final-weight budget

The constraint is **all required deployed weight artifacts together <250,000,000 decimal bytes**: backbone, all deployed heads, adapters, quantization parameters and external tensor shards. Tokenizer/config/metadata sizes should be reported separately. Parameter counts and advertised precision do not prove compliance. Training checkpoints, optimizer states, activations and peak RAM are separate budgets; quantized serving does not imply training directly in INT8/Q4 or fitting training into 250 MB.

## From source audit to scoped training targets

Detailed fields and short annotator rules are in [family contracts](synthetic-data.md#family-contracts). They distinguish semantic targets from deterministic settlement and from experimental proposals.

1. **Search direction:** previous question/results-availability and complete proposed query → SAME_HUNT / NEW_DIRECTION / UNKNOWN. Not outgoing relevance, command authorization, or search-result usefulness.
2. **Required output:** untouched task and exact subject → REQUIRED_OUTPUT / NOT_REQUIRED_OUTPUT / UNKNOWN plus supporting task span. Existing required-output judgment is the convergence target; do not add a second filename heuristic.
3. **Exact form:** untouched task and exact counted-file identity → EXACT_FILE_FORM / NO_EXACT_FILE_FORM / UNKNOWN with exact byte/newline fields. Deterministic code checks literal support and measures current bytes; the classifier never “does arithmetic by prose.”
4. **Intake:** complete latest request and sufficient public referents → QUESTION / SIMPLE / TASK / UNKNOWN. This seed scopes the engagement question only; dominant work type is a separate possible head. No planner enablement follows.
5. **Compaction request:** full latest turn, tool-menu availability → YES / NO / UNKNOWN about summary-for-continuation intent, not whether summary would help.
6. **Retrospection:** entire briefing → PLAN / RETROSPECTIVE / UNKNOWN about new operative instructions, not truth/fidelity. Keep the existing runtime fail-closed handling separate from unknown training labels.
7. **Dictation:** entire public directive → DICTATES / DESCRIBES / UNKNOWN about prescribed copyable implementation text, not action safety, executability, or correctness.
8. **Whole file:** complete candidate payload, presentation and target facts → WHOLE_FILE / OTHER / UNKNOWN about how content is presented, not compiling successfully, line count, or write permission.
9. **Runtime E2E requirement:** untouched task and requested interface → E2E_REQUIRED / NOT_REQUIRED / UNKNOWN about required execution-based end-to-end validation. Not whether tests actually ran or whether completion is justified.
10. **Deferred public progress research:** first establish sufficient bounded chronological public action/result evidence and an independently reviewed label contract. No progress/rumination examples or selected label schema are exported in this seed; author sketches were withheld rather than treating summary-like observations as gold. This is not a private-rumination detector, mental-state diagnosis, or replacement for the current guard. Do not train/export private reasoning, even when it was useful for analysis.

## Staged experiment design (not executed)

1. **Audit first.** Freeze the authoritative archive cutoff. Preserve official model/language/task/harness joins and explicit unknowns; keep failures, no-fit, missing-public-output and unlabeled eligible cases visible. Inventory counts are not adjudicated training counts. Confirm rights, secrets/privacy and public/private channels before export. The archive audit owns the measured gaps; the seed's taxonomy is not an estimate of those gaps.
2. **Split before annotation-driven augmentation.** Group connected records by root task/scenario, source session/run, forks/refolds, repeated prompts/templates and duplicate ancestry before allocating TRAIN / DEV / locked TEST. Add a future-time/scenario challenge if enough data exists. Exact hashes and deterministic near-duplicate candidates aid review; semantic duplicates require review. No row-level random splits across variants. Preserve missing grouping metadata as unknown; quarantine uncertain ancestry instead of asserting independence.
3. **Adjudicate.** Two independent reviewers label a stratified initial real sample without historical verdicts/teacher explanations; resolve disagreements with exact policy and support spans, or retain UNKNOWN. Separate true ambiguity from missing evidence, unsupported language and no-fit. Preserve label-policy revisions and disagreement rate. Never overwrite the historical raw verdict with new gold. Give false permission/release examples explicit review priority, without making an arbitrary numeric cost matrix the operator never approved.
4. **Augment only from TRAIN.** This standalone seed has no archive split lineage and is quarantined for review. Once a real split exists, new archive-derived synthetic work may use only reviewed TRAIN parents. Translations, paraphrases, counterfactuals, generator batches and their near duplicates inherit one train-only leakage group. Match measured gaps in programming ecosystem, human language, task, style/harness and semantic phenomenon; do not just translate Java templates. Keep real and synthetic provenance distinct and compare real-only, seed-only diagnostic and mixed-data arms. No evaluation inputs, rationales or locked templates in generation prompts.
5. **Learning curves and active learning.** Compare increasing numbers of independent real groups, not merely rows, over multiple fixed seeds. Review disagreements/uncertainty on an unlabeled TRAIN pool; keep a separately random sample to detect selection bias. DEV can choose training hyperparameters; an independently reserved calibration portion of DEV can choose abstention thresholds. Do not repeatedly inspect locked TEST to choose examples, thresholds or hypotheses. No fixed sample quota or accuracy guarantee follows from a few-shot paper.
6. **Measure decisions and abstention separately.** Report each family/class, real versus synthetic, programming/human language separately, task/harness/scenario groups, evidence length/position and phenomenon. Report complete-input coverage, NO_FIT, missing evidence, parser/runtime errors and exact ties separately from covered-subset accuracy. Evaluate group-level counterfactual consistency, false release/permission rates, precision/recall and macro metrics with uncertainty over independent groups. Calibrated abstention means selecting a risk/coverage operating point on separate validation data and checking calibration/Brier or reliability plots; softmax is not confidence by decree. A false WHOLE_FILE allowance, missed PLAN, unsupported required-output claim and mistaken new-search permission do not have identical costs.
7. **Shadow before admission.** After authorization, compare frozen candidate heads with the current production judgments on complete real public inputs, recording disagreements without changing actions. Independently inspect errors, time/memory and fit; quantify where another reasoner call is avoided versus where uncertainty remains. Only explicit operator-approved, source-tested per-seat admission can change runtime. UNKNOWN/NO_FIT keeps the existing safe evidence/judgment path—not a new lexical substitute, automatic approval, or human handback. Planning, disabled step preparation/incoming spill-read judgment, and deleted outgoing relevance stay unchanged.

(2025), [paper v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.11412v1), complete paper including appendices; [author 68M card](https://huggingface.co/jhu-clsp/ettin-encoder-68m); inspected [68M config](https://huggingface.co/jhu-clsp/ettin-encoder-68m/raw/main/config.json) and [150M config](https://huggingface.co/jhu-clsp/ettin-encoder-150m/raw/main/config.json). MIT card; exact redistribution/license must accompany a future pinned export.
- **modernbert2024:** Warner et al. (2024), [paper v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.13663v1), complete paper/appendices. Architecture, code and weights released Apache-2.0 according to Appendix G.
- **setfit2022:** Tunstall et al. (2022), [complete paper rendering](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2209.11055), [author explanation](https://huggingface.co/blog/setfit). The method is contrastive adaptation plus classification, not free-text synthetic generation.
- **zerogen2022:** Ye et al. (2022), [complete paper rendering](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2202.07922), [author implementation README](https://github.com/jiacheng-ye/ZeroGen). No external examples imported.
- **universal-nli2023:** Laurer et al., [complete paper rendering](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2312.17543), especially held-out-task evaluation and negative-transfer limits. Its “universal” terminology is the paper's framing, not a claim that all our judgments can be replaced.

- **label-nli-card:** [author README](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tasksource/zero-shot-label-nli/raw/main/README.md), read completely. Per-source licenses and revisions remain material; no blanket permission inferred.
- **hans2019:** McCoy, Pavlick and Linzen, [author repository README](https://github.com/tommccoy1/hans), complete README; *Right for the Wrong Reasons: Diagnosing Syntactic Heuristics in Natural Language Inference*. Used for method/schema descriptions, not newly measured benchmark scores.
- **anli2020:** Nie et al., [author repository README](https://github.com/facebookresearch/anli), complete README including verifier/split/license notes; *Adversarial NLI: A New Benchmark for Natural Language Understanding*. No benchmark data imported.
- **checklist2020:** Ribeiro et al. (2020), [complete paper rendering](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2005.04118), especially §§2, 3 and 5. Behavioral tests complement real held-out evidence and do not certify security.

- **calibration2017:** Guo et al. (2017), [primary paper rendering](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1706.04599), especially §§2–5. Validation-fitted calibration does not certify distribution-shift robustness or semantic correctness.
- **synthetic-modernbert2024:** Berenstein (2024), [complete author tutorial](https://huggingface.co/blog/davidberenstein1957/fine-tune-modernbert-on-synthetic-data). Community tutorial, not peer-reviewed generalization evidence; its reported number is not adopted as a target.
