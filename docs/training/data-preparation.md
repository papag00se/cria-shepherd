## Why these targets, and what they do not solve

The active targets are two previously demonstrated decision problems, not arbitrary code modules:

- **Retrospective state versus operative repair instructions.** A briefing must preserve original goals, unmet outcomes, observed errors and historical work without adding a new implementation recipe. Both missed plans and false accusations against quoted historical code matter.
- **Quoted reality versus dictated implementation.** A diagnosis may name existing functions and quote errors or current code. It must not hand the coder unverified literal code, replacement calls or commands to copy. Both missed dictation and unjustified blocking of factual diagnoses matter.

Availability alone does not establish impact. These classifiers are not established remedies for false completion, stalled work, poor implementation or low usefulness. Whole-task completion needs its complete task/work/check evidence; the earlier long satisfaction input cannot be shortened into a candidate-only surrogate. Stalled-work recognition still needs a sufficient bounded public evidence packet and adjudicated outcomes; the withdrawn public-progress sketches remain withdrawn. Search-direction reversals and normalized-equality bypass remain separate recorded opportunities, not fixes achieved by either active head. Missing upstream inputs must be repaired upstream, not learned away. Execution, permissions, freshness, wire integrity and ownership remain deterministic.

Other seed families are **not** silently promoted into additional training heads because they are easy to label. The entire deployed bundle, including all distinct backbones and heads, must eventually fit below 250,000,000 weight bytes. No production repair or removed search-relevance mechanism is enabled here.

## Review and admission

Two isolated assistant reviews judge each complete input under the exact question policy. The original pilot has Main plus an independent reviewer; the extension has separate primary and independent reviewers. They do actual label/privacy review, not peer synchronization. Review identities, complete-input hashes, literal support spans and original annotations remain separately inspectable. Supporting spans are annotation receipts, **not** shorter replacement model inputs.

Agreement is a provisional reviewed label, **not human-certified gold or statistically independent evidence**: reviewers from the same model family can share errors. No majority or automatic Main override resolves disagreement. UNKNOWN, a reviewer hold, unapproved local use, conflicting partitions or native no-fit keeps the case quarantined. Past unexecuted intent, prescription by reference and instructions addressed to the briefing author are recorded policy-boundary problems rather than forced labels.

Main inspected all complete pilot inputs for local use. Extension reviewers inspect complete inputs for local use and explicitly record approval/holds. Potential embedded private deliberation, live wallet/stake identifiers and other unresolved sensitive provenance are not exported into training inputs or targets. Local-only approval is not anonymity, permission for external redistribution or universal privacy/legal certification. Original generated-suite paths are not silently rewritten. Released repository metadata contains hashes/counts rather than captured prose or sensitive support excerpts.

The immutable 72-row synthetic source remains a proposal ledger, with its original pending status and source hash. The separate review record covers all 72: 45 English determinate labels are reviewed for training-only use; 18 multilingual proposals await translation review/certification; nine unresolved or unavailable-evidence cases remain quarantined. Only 11 reviewed English examples belong to the two active questions and qualify for their training export. The other 34 reviewed English labels are inactive, not extra targets or evaluation examples. The corrected calendar-reference case retains its original proposal history; it is not reported as an untouched author/reviewer agreement.

The four active synthetic scenario groups were inspected against the six real task families: calendar merge, raster conversion, telemetry units and weather-client backoff do not reproduce the shipping, cart, orders-service, importer, handle-resolver or TOML-lookup scenarios. This is a bounded near-duplicate review, not proof of global semantic independence. No held-out archive example is an augmentation source. Synthetic siblings remain training-only; adding further archive-derived variations requires partitioning their ancestry first.

## Leakage-safe partitions, not a fresh benchmark

The task-family partition is fixed across **both** heads, coders, sessions, retries/refolds and exact-input aliases:

- **Training:** supplier importer, orders service and shipping module.
- **External development:** cart billing.
- **Task-held-out diagnostic evaluation:** handle resolver and TOML CLI.

All observed native task variants stay within their canonical original task family. Exact inputs, session IDs and run IDs add connected-component constraints; a component that would cross the fixed assignments is withheld, not split. Multi-cell campaign membership by itself is not a shared-scenario edge. Grouping all versions of each original task conservatively keeps its historical reruns/refolds together. This is not a certification that every unretained ancestor is known: unresolved task lineage remains ineligible, and the original independent-lineage certification stays UNKNOWN.

These are only six previously inspected archive task families, all canonically joined to Codex. Evaluation is therefore **known-archive, task-held-out diagnostics**, not fresh representative traffic, harness generalization, independent samples per row or an untouched population benchmark. Prior replay controls remain exposed diagnostics. A fresh representative real benchmark remains a deployment-evaluation blocker.

Cart billing alone has only one eligible dictation input, so it cannot supply two-class calibration. Before any new inference, a three-fold task-group cross-fit contract was frozen inside the training partition: leave one entire training task out, fit on the other two, and pool out-of-fold predictions for calibration. **Both heads/shared encoder must exclude the held-out task in that fold**; holding it out from only one head would leak it through the other. Admitted standalone synthetic training groups may remain training inputs, but calibration/evaluation archive cases may not generate synthetic examples. Fit/calibrate no threshold on the final diagnostic evaluation set. Final fitting may use all admitted training groups only after the calibration rule is fixed.

The separate 20-case hard-boundary contract deliberately tests historical attribution, non-imperative repair advice, original unmet outcomes, quoted code, exact new commands, cosmetic rewrite invariance and invalid-looking-but-still-dictated code. These are a disclosed subset of the same held-out real pool, **not 20 additional independent samples**. Report its results separately without double-counting or tuning on them. Consequential class misses and false positives matter more than one overall accuracy number.

## Native inputs and fit

The future native head receives the **entire raw candidate string**, with its native tokenizer special tokens—not a chat transcript, generated JSON answer, selected passage, rationale or support span. `complete_input` retains the original structured evidence; only absent optional null references carry no additional supplied text. Any additional non-null evidence makes this single-text adapter ineligible until a new complete packet is defined.

Tokenizer-only measurement pins `tasksource/ModernBERT-base-nli` at `de4ab7e77845098b7fab7f6ab9d370ddff27b19c` and its actual configuration limit of 2048. It measures all 738 real inputs plus 16 active-family synthetic proposals without loading model weights, running inference or accessing the GPU. Of those annotation-stage inputs, 753 fit and one needs 2454 tokens. The latter is NO_FIT, not clipped or chunk-voted. This is **not** a model/backbone selection. A subsequent separately pinned native-tokenizer measurement of Ettin68 at `ac19ae4bc51093b31c475665ac872a936d056cc2` measured the same 754 complete annotation-stage inputs: all fit its actual 7999-position limit, maximum 2454. The admitted four split files are byte-identical across profiles. The formerly oversized example still has a packet-origin hold; additional window capacity does not release it or resolve uncertain provenance.

`python -m suite.training_admit ... --native-fit ...` verifies complete review coverage, input/support custody, local-use approval, question-specific labels, the synthetic source contract, disjoint group identities and exact tokenizer measurement bindings. It exports only fitting, admitted records into separate real training, synthetic training, external development and diagnostic evaluation files. Quarantine metadata preserves all withheld identities/reasons. Numeric class order is frozen: retrospective `[RETROSPECTIVE, PLAN]`; dictation `[DESCRIBES, DICTATES]`. No original teacher rationale or assistant annotation becomes a model feature or target.

## Readiness and remaining blockers

The release record distinguishes **completed label/data preparation** from permission and evidence required for actual training. There has been no training, classifier inference experiment, GPU access/clearance, live-model swap, production configuration/service change or campaign change.

The [first recipe](training-recipe.json) now freezes the chosen initial checkpoint/tokenizer/heads, data, folds, ablations, seeds, sampling/loss/optimizer, calibration and error reporting. The [resource record](resource-plan.json) includes native fit and a CPU-only meta-device constructor check of all encoder/head shapes. This substantiates tensor arithmetic, not peak VRAM, serialized serving bytes or CPU latency. There is no backbone winner or implemented/validated trainer/export command.

Remaining boundaries:

1. Supply an approved source of new, unrelated real task sessions, reserve ancestry before capture, and apply the [prospective collection contract](fresh-evaluation.json). **Fresh cases/groups currently equal zero**; known archive diagnostics cannot fill that gap.
2. Keep all unresolved labels/provenance private and quarantined. Their exclusion is not evidence that the hard problem disappeared. Other high-severity problems listed above remain outside these two heads.
3. Implement/validate the trainer/exporter and verify the published pretrained checkpoint before execution. A dedicated CPU-only constructor environment exists; GPU runtime/resource compatibility remains unmeasured. No training driver is claimed to exist.
4. Before any deployment claim, measure the actual complete bundle below 250,000,000 bytes, unquantized/quantized errors, CPU compatibility, latency and RSS, plus a fresh representative evaluation. Proposed tensor bytes are not certification.
5. Notify the operator when execution readiness is concrete, then **wait for explicit separate clearance** before clearing the current GPU workload or starting training. No preparation, package install or CPU-only shape construction grants that permission.
