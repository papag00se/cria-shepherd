## Split and evidence contract

Assign the source/project/template family before authoring a requirement or its variants. Every sibling, translation, derivative and near-clone stays in that family's split. The explicitly `train-`-prefixed families in `suite/progress_correctness/` were assigned to TRAIN before their implementations were written. DEV and TEST must use separately originated families. Reserve **a distinct author-controlled family namespace for TEST**, disjoint from TRAIN and DEV: `heldout-author/`. The held-out author must choose and record actual project families before authoring and must not expose their payload to TRAIN/DEV construction or quality tuning. No TEST implementation or payload has been authored here. A namespace reservation is not yet a substantive held-out family inventory; coordinate that inventory with the isolated author without opening its cases.

A group records the requirement, an unmet seed, three snapshots and independently written executable checks. The complete snapshot must pass all checks; a partial snapshot must make real progress and fail the intended clause while passing other relevant checks. The unchanged or unrelated-edit snapshot must not deliver the requirement. Execution is evidence, not an independent semantic review of whether the checks match every requirement. Exclude examples whose inputs cannot support the judgment, including requirements dependent on unseen behavior or unprovided measurements. No labels, verifier programs/results, repair rationales, internal stage or private reasoning go into classifier input.

The blind view provides the unmodified policy (`cria/prompts/offline_onejev_progress.txt`), the existing `render_state` fields, the text produced by the existing System 1 state renderer and the available label options. The diff uses complete context for changed text files rather than the historical two-line diff; this is necessary so unchanged portions of a changed seed file are not hidden, and avoids the existing large-file/binary summaries. Current seeds have no implementation file, so all implementation code appears as an addition. Do not claim visibility of unchanged helper files; future cases must include them in the visible diff or be self-contained. No classifier, tokenizer, classifier inference, checkpoint, or GPU is used. The review packet is shuffled with opaque IDs; its companion verification file must not accompany blind review.

## Running and inspecting

From the repository root run `python -m suite.progress_correctness.build`. Outputs are private under `~/.cria/training/progress-correctness-v2/`: `train.jsonl` (intended labels, full evidence and lineage), `pilot-review-blind.jsonl` (complete policy and state with opaque IDs, no targets), `verification.jsonl` (verifier programs, outputs, rationale and mapping), and `report.json` (counts, exact duplicates, character lengths and cross-family similarity candidates). IDs persist on rerun for the same group/label; if any content changes, a reviewer must be given the updated packet, not assumed to have reviewed it. Run `python -m suite.progress_correctness.compare_train` after building to collect lexical candidates and exact rendered-input matches against the historical progress TRAIN dataset only; this writes `historical-near-candidates.json`. Similarity is a candidate list, not semantic truth or an automatic quarantine decision.

Fixtures execute sequentially, each snapshot in a fresh disposable directory beneath `$TMPDIR` or `/var/tmp`, with ten-second subprocess timeouts, no installations or network use by the authored checks, and complete captured stdout/stderr. Missing executable and timeout are recorded, not treated as a pass. Checks are independent assertions of behavior, not source spelling; read the requirements and check programs before trusting the proposed labels.

## Historical DEV erratum

The historical `interval-clj` DEV `total-length/done` and `gaps/done` snapshots retain a `merge-intervals` seed helper that uses `[la b]`, not the maximum endpoint. On a static trace, contained intervals `[[1 10] [2 3]]` merge incorrectly to `[[1 3]]`: `covered-length` yields 2 rather than 9, and adding `[12 14]` yields gap `[3 12]` rather than `[10 12]`. This is a static source finding, not an executed Clojure check: the reviewing environment lacked Clojure and bb. The historical fixtures and labels remain untouched; published historical scores are not silently rescored. New labels cannot rely on hidden or broken seed helpers.

The reviewer’s suggestion that the classifier is a “looks finished” detector is a hypothesis, not an established cause. Conflicting historical targets do not establish why annotators produced them. Review length and defect-mix ratios as diagnostics, not acceptance gates or empirical laws.

## Current observations and next work

The pilot contains 50 groups in 14 synthetic families: 114 Python, 21 JavaScript and 15 Ruby examples, balanced 50 per class. Every group is three related snapshots, not three independent tasks. Examples include file persistence, cross-file CLI wiring, test coverage explicitly requested in a requirement, boundary conditions and error propagation; the private report carries per-family and defect counts. Many cases remain short one-function projects with addition-only diffs and repeated family-level unrelated features; even though the families were assigned before authoring, they are not representative of large real projects. Several correct solutions use terse or unusual idioms, but independent semantic review is needed to determine whether every proposed label is warranted.

All 300 fixture checks ran without timeout or missing toolchain: the 50 DONE snapshots each passed both checks; every PARTIAL passed the ordinary check and failed its intended omission check; every NOT_STARTED failed both. Those outcomes do not themselves prove that the checks cover each entire requirement or that an attempted implementation deserves PARTIAL. Fourteen NOT_STARTED snapshots are no-ops and 36 carry unrelated changes, including executable features; some DONE and PARTIAL snapshots also contain unrelated features. In the 50 matched pairs, DONE evidence is longer in 25 and PARTIAL in 25. Median complete rendered-state character lengths are 529.5 DONE, 525.5 PARTIAL and 426 NOT_STARTED; these are diagnostics, not thresholds or token counts.

In the original pilot there were no exact duplicate rendered inputs, and no exact matches against the 3,770 historical progress TRAIN rows scanned. The highest five-token evidence-shingle similarity to historical TRAIN is about 0.304; lexical candidates, including across pilot families, remain unadjudicated and are not a semantic independence claim. No examples were excluded at pilot construction. The original and both revisions are retained separately in the private training directory; `revision-changes.json` in each revision maps changed inputs and IDs. Review artifacts are under `review/`. Full verifier programs and their outputs, author repair rationales, and the ID/label mapping are private; send only the blind view to blind reviewers.

The reviewed pilot still has serious shortcuts: all 150 diffs are additions only, every NOT_STARTED avoids the requested file, and 45 of 50 DONE/PARTIAL pairs have exactly one repeated distractor. Most PARTIAL snapshots are complete-looking implementations with one defect; there are no alternative DONE siblings. Do not scale that template. The independent tax revision's >100-percent domain is explicitly synthetic, not a claim about typical taxes.

Historical, unreviewed expansion packets are separately stored at `expansion-review-01/`: 100 TRAIN rows from 33 groups in 18 assigned families (one additional DONE alternative), and 12 initial DEV rows from four preassigned families. These include edits to existing seed files, same-file unrelated NOT_STARTED edits, deletions, integration and required-test cases. Their 242 fixture checks ran. Subsequent lexical inspection found strong cross-split template similarity, including the initial DEV flag-report and multiple TRAIN two-function cases; all four initial DEV groups are quarantined pending semantic review, despite disjoint family IDs. They are not clean DEV evidence.

A second DEV batch contains three groups; one CSV-import group is also quarantined due to a cross-split similarity candidate involving the community TRAIN CSV-import case. The remaining two groups / six rows are in `expansion-review-03/dev-blind.jsonl` for independent blind review, not yet admitted DEV. Their 12 checks ran with expected outcomes. This packet's report preserves the excluded IDs and includes lexical candidates against expansion TRAIN and the reviewed pilot; no exact duplicates appear within those batches. Similarity scores are review leads, not semantic decisions. The 100 TRAIN proposals remain at `expansion-review-01/train-blind.jsonl`; their considerable weakness is repeated two-function partials, not 33 independent fully developed projects.
